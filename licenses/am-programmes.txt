Arroyo Fold original AM radio programmes

radio-weather-original.ogg: original fictional evening valley bulletin from weather.txt,
spoken locally with Piper en_US-ljspeech-high. The LJSpeech source dataset is public domain; model card: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/high/MODEL_CARD
Piper software: GPL-3.0, used as a build tool; no Piper runtime/model ships in the app.
The bulletin is fictional in-world programming, not a current forecast.

radio-music-original.ogg: an original seeded 32-bar instrumental generated by compose.py.
No recordings, samples, lyrics, or third-party compositions are used.

Generate: python compose.py; python -m piper -m en_US-ljspeech-high.onnx -i weather.txt -f weather.wav
Encode: ffmpeg -i INPUT.wav -af highpass=f=170,lowpass=f=3400 -c:a libvorbis -q:a 3 OUTPUT.ogg

native-radio.py installs editable equivalents into the open Blender native scene.
Runtime controls, broadcast loops and light behavior live in work/web/car-radio.js.
