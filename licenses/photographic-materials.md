The architecture, interiors and landscaping remain original. These assets contain isolated photographic surface scans only; no photograph of a building or room is used.

All five scan sources were checked on the official asset pages and the Poly Haven license page on 2026-11-18. They are CC0-1.0. Exact official download URLs, source resolution, authors and matching MD5 hashes are preserved in manifest.json.
