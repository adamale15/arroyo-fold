# Photographic site and architectural finishes

The daytime granite horizon, sky, and linear radiance measurements derive from Greg Zaal’s Klippad Sunrise 2. The late-night upper sky derives from his Rogland Moonlit Night. The camera moves through modeled terrain, a planted garden and an original house.

https://polyhaven.com/a/klippad_sunrise_2  
https://polyhaven.com/a/rogland_moonlit_night

Stacked Stone Wall supplies color, roughness, normal and relief for the split-face feature wall. Concrete Floor 03 supplies the patio surface. The terrazzo uses a seamless crop made from the interior of the Terrazzo Tiles scan.
